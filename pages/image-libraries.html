ImageLibraries — Free Donationware SVG Stamps

ImageLibraries is our growing collection of free rubber-stamp style graphics in SVG format. Download transparent vector images for presentations, websites, social posts, and print projects. Everything is donationware: use it freely, and donate if it saves you time.

Why SVG stamp graphics?

SVG files stay sharp at any size, load fast on the web, and are easy to recolor in design tools. Each library groups phrases by theme; every phrase ships in multiple vintage stamp styles so you can pick the look that fits your brand.
