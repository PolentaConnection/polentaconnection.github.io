Polenta ToDo Wallpaper

Download Portable Download Installer

Description

Polenta ToDo Wallpaper shows your daily to-do list as a panel on the Windows desktop. It stays behind your applications like a wallpaper, lets mouse clicks pass through by default, and is controlled from the system tray.

Platform: Windows 10/11 (x64)
License: Freeware
Version: 1.0.0

Features

  • Desktop wallpaper layer
    Embedded on the Windows desktop, behind other windows and icons.
  • Click-through by default
    Your desktop and apps stay fully usable until you enable interaction.
  • System tray control
    No taskbar button or Alt+Tab entry — manage the app from the notification area.
  • To-do list
    Add, edit, and check off tasks with Edit and Save modes.
  • Customizable look
    Adjust colors, font size, opacity, window size and position.
  • Show / hide
    Hide the panel temporarily without closing the application.

Data storage

Settings and your to-do list are saved in settings.json next to the application executable. Your data stays on your PC.
