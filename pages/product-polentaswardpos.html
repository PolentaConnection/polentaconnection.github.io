Polenta Swardpos

Download Portable Download Installer

Description

Polenta Swardpos helps you regain access to your own password-protected documents and archives when you still remember pieces of the password—names, dates, places, habits—but not the exact spelling or order.

You define word groups and transformation rules (capitalization, reversed words, optional characters, combinations). Polenta Swardpos builds a focused candidate list from that knowledge, then verifies candidates against files you select on your PC. Everything runs locally and offline: no cloud upload, no account required.

Built for personal data recovery, IT support, and digital forensics workflows where you already have a legitimate right to open the file—Microsoft Office, LibreOffice / OpenDocument, ZIP (ZipCrypto and AES-256), RAR, and 7z.

Platform: Windows 10/11 (x64)
License: Freeware
Version: 1.0.0

Intended use

Polenta Swardpos is a forgotten-password recovery utility for documents and archives that belong to you or that you are authorized to unlock. It is not designed to open other people’s files without permission. Use it when a partial memory of the password is enough to rebuild likely candidates.

Features

  • Rule-based candidate lists
    Combine remembered words with capitalization, word order, prefixes, suffixes and optional characters into a precise list—far more efficient than guessing at random.
  • Reusable recovery profiles
    Save named profiles so the same word groups and rules can be reused across related files or later attempts.
  • Wide document and archive support
    Microsoft Office, LibreOffice/OpenDocument (ODF), ZIP (ZipCrypto and AES-256), RAR and 7z—verified on your machine.
  • Generate list or verify on file
    Export the full candidate list beside the source file for review, or run verification and stop when the matching password is found (sequential or shuffled order).
  • Clear local results
    Writes {file}.AllPasswords.txt for generated lists and {file}.Password.txt when recovery succeeds—next to the document you selected.
  • Private by design
    No telemetry cloud, no online dictionary download required. Portable zip or Windows installer; profiles stay on your PC.

Formats

  • Microsoft Office: DOC, DOCX, XLS, XLSX, PPT, PPTX (password-protected)
  • LibreOffice / OpenDocument: ODT, ODS, ODP, ODG, ODF
  • Archives: ZIP (ZipCrypto and AES-256), RAR, 7z

Data storage

Recovery profiles are stored as *.profile.json on your PC. Where they live depends on how you run the app:

  • Portable download: next to the executable. The zip includes PolentaSwardpos.deploy.json with portable mode.
  • Installer: under %LOCALAPPDATA%\Polenta Connection\PolentaSwardpos. The installer sets installed mode automatically.

Your profiles and results stay on your computer.
