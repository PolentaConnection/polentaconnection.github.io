Polenta Meal Planner

Download Portable Download Installer

Description

Polenta Meal Planner helps you plan your daily meals on Windows. Maintain a personal food database, build menus for breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks, and see calories, protein, carbs and fats in real time.

Platform: Windows 10/11 (x64)
License: Freeware
Version: 1.0.0

Features

  • Food database
    Store foods with macros per 100g, brands, default portions and notes.
  • Daily meal planner
    Organize meals by time of day with drag-and-drop from the food list.
  • Macro totals
    Per-meal and daily totals for kcal, protein, carbs, fats and saturated fat.
  • Filter & copy
    Quick food search (Ctrl+F), copy daily totals or the full menu to the clipboard.
  • Export
    Export a formatted daily menu to a text file.
  • Themes
    Bootstrap 5 and classic Windows-inspired looks.

Data storage

Foods and menus are saved as XML files in Foods and Menus folders next to the application executable. Your data stays on your PC.
